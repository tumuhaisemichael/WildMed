Best Wildlife Viewing Seasons

Dry Season (June - October)

  • Peak wildlife viewing as animals gather at water sources
  • Great Migration river crossings in northern Serengeti
  • Clear skies and excellent visibility

Green Season (November - May)

  • Calving season in southern Serengeti (Jan-Feb)
  • Lush landscapes with dramatic skies
  • Fewer crowds and attractive rates

Essential Travel Information

Plan confidently with practical details for Tanzania travel

Visa & Entry

  • Tourist visas available online or on arrival
  • Passport validity: minimum 6 months
  • Yellow fever certificate required if arriving from endemic regions

Health & Safety

  • Malaria prophylaxis recommended
  • Travel insurance required for trekking and safaris
  • Stay hydrated and use insect repellent

Climate

  • Warm days and cool nights in the north
  • Dry season: June-October (best for safaris)
  • Wet seasons: March-May and November-December

Currency & Payments

  • Tanzanian Shilling (TZS)
  • USD widely accepted in tourist areas
  • Card payments in major hotels and lodges

Language & Culture

  • Swahili and English are widely spoken
  • Friendly hospitality and rich cultural heritage
  • Respect local customs and dress modestly in towns

Packing Tips

  • Neutral-colored clothing for game drives
  • Light jacket for early mornings
  • Binoculars, sunscreen, and insect repellent
