5-Day The Best of Kisoro Tour: Gorillas, Culture & Lake Bunyonyi

5-Day The Best of Kisoro Tour

Gorilla trekking, Batwa culture, golden monkeys, and serene Lake Bunyonyi in one unforgettable private tour

1

Day 1: Arrival & Transfer to Kisoro

Entebbe to Kisoro

Arrival & Journey

  • • Arrive at Entebbe International Airport
  • • Meet your personal guide from WildMed
  • • Scenic drive through Southwest Uganda
  • • Views of lush green hills and local life

Accommodation & Evening

  • • Check into Ichumbi Hotel
  • • Unwind after your journey
  • • Dinner at the lodge
  • • Briefing about the days ahead

Travel Insight

Kisoro is nestled near the borders of Rwanda and DRC, surrounded by volcanic mountains. The drive offers spectacular views of Uganda's famous terraced hills.

2

Day 2: Gorilla Trekking in Mgahinga

Mgahinga Gorilla National Park

Gorilla Trekking Experience

  • • Early breakfast at the lodge
  • • Transfer to Mgahinga Gorilla National Park
  • • Briefing by park rangers
  • • Trek to find mountain gorilla family
  • • 1 hour with the gentle giants

Afternoon & Photography

  • • Return to lodge for lunch
  • • Rest and relaxation time
  • • Review photos and memories
  • • Optional village walk in evening

Expert Guidance

Mgahinga is one of only three parks in the world where you can see endangered mountain gorillas. Our expert guides ensure a safe and unforgettable encounter.

3

Day 3: Batwa Culture & Coffee Experience

Cultural Immersion Day

Batwa Cultural Trail

  • • Visit the indigenous Batwa people
  • • Learn about ancient forest lifestyle
  • • Traditional medicine demonstration
  • • Storytelling and cultural exchange
  • • Support community tourism

Coffee Farm Experience

  • • Visit local coffee farm
  • • Hands-on harvesting experience
  • • Learn roasting techniques
  • • Taste Uganda's famed Arabica coffee
  • • Purchase fresh coffee beans

Cultural Significance

The Batwa are the original inhabitants of these forests. This experience helps preserve their heritage while providing sustainable income for the community.

4

Day 4: Golden Monkeys & Lake Bunyonyi

Kisoro to Lake Bunyonyi

Golden Monkey Tracking

  • • Morning golden monkey tracking
  • • See these playful primates in bamboo
  • • Photography opportunities
  • • Learn about conservation efforts

Lake Bunyonyi Transfer

  • • Scenic drive to Lake Bunyonyi
  • • Check into Arcadia Lodge
  • • Relaxing boat ride on the lake
  • • Visit "Place of Many Birds" islands

Scenic Beauty

Lake Bunyonyi is Africa's second deepest lake, with 29 islands and stunning scenery. It's known for its tranquility and birdlife - perfect for relaxation.

5

Day 5: Lake Sunrise & Return to Entebbe

Lake Bunyonyi to Entebbe

Morning at Lake Bunyonyi

  • • Sunrise views over the lake
  • • Final breakfast at lakeside
  • • Optional morning canoe ride
  • • Photography of misty landscapes

Return Journey

  • • Begin return journey to Entebbe
  • • Stop for lunch en route
  • • Scenic drive through countryside
  • • Drop-off at airport or hotel

Departure Notes

Schedule flights for evening (after 6pm) to allow comfortable travel time. We recommend overnight in Entebbe if you have early morning flights.

Gorilla Trekking Preparation Guide

Essential information for your mountain gorilla adventure

Essential Documents

  • • Valid passport (6+ months remaining)
  • • Uganda visa (available on arrival USD $50)
  • • Yellow fever vaccination certificate
  • • Travel insurance with medical evacuation
  • • Gorilla permit confirmation copy

Medical Requirements

  • • Yellow fever vaccination (mandatory)
  • • Malaria prophylaxis recommended
  • • COVID-19 vaccination recommended
  • • Medical fitness for hiking at altitude
  • • Emergency contact information

Trekking Gear

  • • Sturdy waterproof hiking boots
  • • Garden gloves (for vegetation)
  • • Walking sticks (provided)
  • • Waterproof daypack
  • • Rain poncho or jacket

Clothing

  • • Long-sleeved shirts (neutral colors)
  • • Long pants (quick-dry material)
  • • Warm layers (fleece/jacket)
  • • Hat with brim for sun/rain
  • • Extra socks (wool recommended)

Essential Items

  • • High SPF sunscreen
  • • Insect repellent (DEET based)
  • • Camera with extra batteries
  • • Binoculars (8x32 ideal)
  • • Personal first aid kit

Physical Preparation

  • • Moderate fitness level required
  • • Trekking can be 2-6 hours round trip
  • • Altitude: 2,200-4,000 meters
  • • Practice hiking with daypack
  • • Stay hydrated before trek

Gorilla Trekking Rules

  • • Maximum 1 hour with gorillas
  • • Stay 7 meters from gorillas
  • • No flash photography
  • • Keep voices low
  • • Follow guide instructions
  • • If sick, do not trek
