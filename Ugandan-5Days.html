5-Day Short Holiday Package: Gorillas, Lions & Lake Bunyonyi

5-Day Short Holiday Package

Queen Elizabeth National Park, Gorilla Trekking & Lake Bunyonyi - The perfect Uganda introduction

1

Day 1: Arrival & Transfer to Queen Elizabeth NP

Entebbe to Queen Elizabeth

Arrival & Journey

  • • Arrive at Entebbe International Airport
  • • Meet your private guide from WildMed
  • • Scenic drive to Queen Elizabeth National Park
  • • Stop for lunch en route

Accommodation & Evening

  • • Check into Irungu Forest Safari Lodge
  • • Unwind and settle in
  • • Dinner at the lodge
  • • Briefing about upcoming safari days

Park Insight

Queen Elizabeth National Park is Uganda's most popular safari destination, known for its diverse wildlife including tree-climbing lions, elephants, and over 600 bird species.

2

Day 2: Game Drive & Kazinga Channel Cruise

Queen Elizabeth NP

Morning Game Drive

  • • Game drive in Ishasha sector
  • • Search for tree-climbing lions
  • • Spot elephants, buffalo, antelopes
  • • Professional guide commentary

Afternoon Boat Cruise

  • • Boat cruise on Kazinga Channel
  • • Hippo and crocodile viewing
  • • Birdwatching (hundreds of species)
  • • Photography opportunities

Wildlife Highlight

The tree-climbing lions of Ishasha are a rare phenomenon - only found in a few places in Africa. Your best chance to see them is during the morning game drive.

3

Day 3: Gorilla Trekking in Bwindi

Bwindi Impenetrable Forest

Gorilla Trekking Experience

  • • Breakfast and travel to Bwindi
  • • Arrive for gorilla trekking briefing
  • • Trek through ancient rainforest
  • • 1 hour with mountain gorilla family

Evening Accommodation

  • • Check into Ride 4 a Woman Guesthouse
  • • Cozy, budget-friendly accommodation
  • • Rest after memorable trek
  • • Share stories of gorilla encounter

Conservation Note

Bwindi Impenetrable Forest is home to almost half of the world's remaining mountain gorillas. Your permit fee directly supports gorilla conservation efforts.

4

Day 4: Scenic Tranquility at Lake Bunyonyi

Bwindi to Lake Bunyonyi

Morning Transfer

  • • Breakfast at guesthouse
  • • Scenic drive to Lake Bunyonyi
  • • Views of terraced hills
  • • Arrive at Itambira Island Resort

Lake Activities

  • • Explore lake islands by boat
  • • Visit local communities
  • • Relaxation by the lakeside
  • • Sunset photography

Scenic Beauty

Lake Bunyonyi means "Place of Many Little Birds" and is Africa's second deepest lake. Its tranquil waters and 29 islands make it perfect for relaxation after safari adventures.

5

Day 5: Lake Views & Return to Entebbe

Lake Bunyonyi to Entebbe

Final Morning

  • • Breakfast overlooking the lake
  • • Last photos of scenic views
  • • Optional morning walk
  • • Pack and prepare for departure

Return Journey

  • • Begin return to Entebbe
  • • Stop for lunch en route
  • • Scenic countryside drive
  • • Airport/hotel drop-off

Departure Notes

Schedule flights for evening (after 6pm) to allow comfortable travel time. We recommend overnight in Entebbe if you have early morning international flights.

Gorilla Trekking Preparation Guide

Essential information for your mountain gorilla adventure

Essential Documents

  • • Valid passport (6+ months remaining)
  • • Uganda visa (available on arrival USD $50)
  • • Yellow fever vaccination certificate
  • • Travel insurance with medical evacuation
  • • Gorilla permit confirmation copy

Medical Requirements

  • • Yellow fever vaccination (mandatory)
  • • Malaria prophylaxis recommended
  • • COVID-19 vaccination recommended
  • • Medical fitness for hiking at altitude
  • • Emergency contact information

Trekking Gear

  • • Sturdy waterproof hiking boots
  • • Garden gloves (for vegetation)
  • • Walking sticks (provided)
  • • Waterproof daypack
  • • Rain poncho or jacket

Clothing

  • • Long-sleeved shirts (neutral colors)
  • • Long pants (quick-dry material)
  • • Warm layers (fleece/jacket)
  • • Hat with brim for sun/rain
  • • Extra socks (wool recommended)

Essential Items

  • • High SPF sunscreen
  • • Insect repellent (DEET based)
  • • Camera with extra batteries
  • • Binoculars (8x32 ideal)
  • • Personal first aid kit

Physical Preparation

  • • Moderate fitness level required
  • • Trekking can be 2-6 hours round trip
  • • Altitude: 2,200-4,000 meters
  • • Practice hiking with daypack
  • • Stay hydrated before trek

Gorilla Trekking Rules

  • • Maximum 1 hour with gorillas
  • • Stay 7 meters from gorillas
  • • No flash photography
  • • Keep voices low
  • • Follow guide instructions
  • • If sick, do not trek
