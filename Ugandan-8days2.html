8-Day Gorilla Safari: Uganda & Rwanda Adventure

8-Day Gorilla Safari: Uganda & Rwanda

Experience the best of East Africa with gorilla trekking in two countries, chimpanzee tracking, game drives, and cultural encounters

1

Day 1: Arrival in Entebbe → Kampala

Entebbe to Kampala

Arrival & Transfer

  • • Arrive at Entebbe International Airport
  • • Warm greeting by your private guide
  • • Transfer to Uganda's capital, Kampala
  • • Settle into your accommodation

Evening & Accommodation

  • • Check into Marie's Royale Hotel
  • • Briefing about your safari adventure
  • • Dinner at the hotel
  • • Rest after your journey

First Night Insight

Kampala is Uganda's vibrant capital, blending modern development with rich cultural heritage. A perfect gentle introduction to East Africa.

2

Day 2: Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary → Murchison Falls

Kampala to Murchison Falls NP

Rhino Encounter

  • • Visit Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary
  • • See Uganda's only wild rhinos
  • • Guided walk with rangers
  • • Photography opportunities

Murchison Falls Journey

  • • Continue to Murchison Falls NP
  • • Check into Nkundwa Nile View Lodge
  • • Evening views of the Nile
  • • Dinner with sunset backdrop

Conservation Insight

Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary is a successful conservation project working to reintroduce rhinos to Uganda's national parks after their extinction in the 1980s.

3

Day 3: Game Drive & Boat Cruise at Murchison Falls

Murchison Falls NP

Morning Game Drive

  • • Early morning game drive
  • • Search for elephants, giraffes
  • • Antelope and buffalo viewing
  • • Birdwatching opportunities

Afternoon Boat Cruise

  • • Relaxing boat cruise on the Nile
  • • Cruise to base of Murchison Falls
  • • Spot hippos and crocodiles
  • • Photography of the waterfall

Natural Wonder

Murchison Falls is where the Nile River forces through a 7-meter gap and plunges 43 meters. The boat cruise offers the best views of this spectacular natural wonder.

4

Day 4: Chimpanzee Tracking in Kibale Forest

Murchison Falls to Kibale Forest

Journey to Kibale

  • • Scenic drive to Kibale National Park
  • • Arrive at Kibale Forest Lodge
  • • Lunch and preparation briefing
  • • Afternoon chimpanzee tracking

Primate Experience

  • • Track chimpanzees in dense forest
  • • 1 hour with chimp family
  • • See other primate species
  • • Evening relaxation at lodge

Primate Capital

Kibale Forest has one of the highest primate densities in the world, with 13 species including the chimpanzees you'll track. The forest is also home to 375 bird species.

5

Day 5: Game Drive in Queen Elizabeth National Park

Kibale to Queen Elizabeth NP

Transfer & Arrival

  • • Morning drive to Queen Elizabeth NP
  • • Check into Pumba Safari Cottages
  • • Lunch with park views
  • • Afternoon relaxation

Evening Game Drive

  • • Late afternoon game drive
  • • Explore savannah plains
  • • Look for tree-climbing lions
  • • Buffalo herds and warthogs

Unique Wildlife

Queen Elizabeth NP is famous for its tree-climbing lions found in the Ishasha sector. These lions are unique as they rest in fig trees during the day.

6

Day 6: Ishasha Sector & Scenic Drive to Bwindi

Queen Elizabeth to Bwindi

Ishasha Sector Exploration

  • • Morning game drive in Ishasha
  • • Search for tree-climbing lions
  • • Photography of unique behavior
  • • Other wildlife spotting

Scenic Highlands Drive

  • • Journey to Bwindi Impenetrable Forest
  • • Drive through scenic highlands
  • • Check into Rushaga Gorilla Lodge
  • • Gorilla trekking briefing

Gorilla Preparation

Tonight's briefing prepares you for tomorrow's gorilla trekking. Learn about gorilla behavior, trekking rules, and what to expect in Bwindi's misty forests.

7

Day 7: Gorilla Trekking in Bwindi → Lake Bunyonyi

Bwindi to Lake Bunyonyi

Gorilla Trekking Experience

  • • Morning gorilla trekking in Bwindi
  • • Find a mountain gorilla family
  • • 1 hour with the gentle giants
  • • Return to lodge for lunch

Lake Bunyonyi Transfer

  • • Travel to Lake Bunyonyi
  • • Check into Itambira Island Resort
  • • Relaxing evening by the lake
  • • Reflect on gorilla experience

Life-Changing Experience

Gorilla trekking in Bwindi is often described as a life-changing experience. The connection with these endangered primates in their natural habitat is unforgettable.

8

Day 8: Cross to Rwanda → Kigali Genocide Memorial

Uganda to Rwanda

Border Crossing

  • • Breakfast with lake views
  • • Cross border into Rwanda
  • • Scenic drive to Kigali
  • • Experience Rwanda's cleanliness

Kigali & Memorial Visit

  • • Visit Kigali Genocide Memorial
  • • Learn about Rwanda's history
  • • Lunch in Kigali city
  • • Airport transfer or hotel drop-off

Journey Conclusion

Your 8-day adventure ends in Rwanda. Schedule flights from Kigali International Airport for evening. The memorial visit offers important historical context to Rwanda's remarkable recovery.

Luxury Safari Preparation Guide

Everything you need for your ultimate 14-day luxury Ugandan adventure

Essential Documents

  • • Valid passport (6+ months remaining)
  • • Uganda visa (available on arrival USD $50)
  • • Yellow fever vaccination certificate
  • • Travel insurance with medical evacuation
  • • Gorilla permit confirmation copy

Medical Requirements

  • • Yellow fever vaccination (mandatory)
  • • Malaria prophylaxis recommended
  • • COVID-19 vaccination recommended
  • • Medical fitness for hiking at altitude
  • • Emergency contact information

Trekking Gear

  • • Sturdy waterproof hiking boots
  • • Garden gloves (for vegetation)
  • • Walking sticks (provided)
  • • Waterproof daypack
  • • Rain poncho or jacket

Clothing

  • • Long-sleeved shirts (neutral colors)
  • • Long pants (quick-dry material)
  • • Warm layers (fleece/jacket)
  • • Hat with brim for sun/rain
  • • Extra socks (wool recommended)

Essential Items

  • • High SPF sunscreen
  • • Insect repellent (DEET based)
  • • Camera with extra batteries
  • • Binoculars (8x32 ideal)
  • • Personal first aid kit

Physical Preparation

  • • Moderate fitness level required
  • • Trekking can be 2-6 hours round trip
  • • Altitude: 2,200-4,000 meters
  • • Practice hiking with daypack
  • • Stay hydrated before trek

Gorilla Trekking Rules

  • • Maximum 1 hour with gorillas
  • • Stay 7 meters from gorillas
  • • No flash photography
  • • Keep voices low
  • • Follow guide instructions
  • • If sick, do not trek
