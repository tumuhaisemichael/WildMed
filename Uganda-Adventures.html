Best Wildlife Viewing Seasons

Dry Season (June - August & December - February)

  • Best gorilla and chimpanzee trekking conditions
  • Better visibility for savanna wildlife
  • Lower rainfall and clearer trails

Green Season (March - May & September - November)

  • Lush landscapes and dramatic skies
  • Great birding with migratory species
  • Fewer crowds and discounted rates

Essential Travel Information

Plan confidently with practical details for Uganda travel

Visa & Entry

  • Tourist visas available online or on arrival
  • Passport validity: minimum 6 months
  • Yellow fever certificate required

Health & Safety

  • Malaria prophylaxis recommended
  • Travel insurance recommended for trekking activities
  • Stay hydrated and use insect repellent

Climate

  • Equatorial climate with mild temperatures
  • Dry seasons: June-August and December-February
  • Wet seasons: March-May and September-November

Currency & Payments

  • Ugandan Shilling (UGX)
  • USD widely accepted for tourism services
  • Card payments in major hotels and lodges

Language & Culture

  • English and Swahili widely spoken
  • Friendly, welcoming communities
  • Respect local customs and dress modestly in towns

Packing Tips

  • Neutral-colored clothing for wildlife viewing
  • Rain jacket for forest walks
  • Insect repellent and sunscreen
