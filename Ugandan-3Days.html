Experience the Magic of Mountain Gorillas

3-Day Gorilla Trekking & Batwa Experience - Perfect for Last-Minute Adventures

3-Day Gorilla Trekking & Batwa Experience

A compact yet powerful itinerary combining gorilla encounters with meaningful cultural immersion

1

Day 1: Journey to Bwindi Impenetrable Forest

Entebbe/Kampala to Bwindi

Scenic Transfer

  • • Pickup from Entebbe Airport or Kampala hotel
  • • Drive through lush green landscapes
  • • Journey to Southwest Uganda
  • • Arrive at Bwindi Impenetrable National Park

Evening at Lodge

  • • Check into Rushaga Gorilla Lodge
  • • Briefing about gorilla trekking
  • • Dinner and overnight stay
  • • Prepare for next day's adventure

Park Insight

Bwindi Impenetrable National Park is home to nearly half the world's remaining mountain gorillas. The park is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

2

Day 2: Gorilla Trekking & Batwa Culture

Bwindi Impenetrable Forest

Gorilla Trekking

  • • Early morning briefing at park headquarters
  • • Guided trek into the impenetrable forest
  • • Find and observe mountain gorilla family
  • • 1 unforgettable hour with the gorillas
  • • Return to lodge for lunch

Batwa Cultural Experience

  • • Afternoon visit to Batwa community
  • • Learn about indigenous forest lifestyle
  • • Traditional dances and storytelling
  • • Cultural exchange with Batwa people
  • • Understand their conservation role

Conservation Note

Your visit supports both gorilla conservation and Batwa community livelihoods. Tourism provides sustainable income for forest protection.

3

Day 3: Return to Kampala/Entebbe

Bwindi to Entebbe/Kampala

Morning at Bwindi

  • • Final breakfast at Rushaga Gorilla Lodge
  • • Last views of the forest canopy
  • • Opportunity for souvenir shopping
  • • Share trekking stories with fellow travelers

Return Journey

  • • Begin return journey with packed lunch
  • • Scenic drive through Ugandan countryside
  • • Stop for photos and refreshments
  • • Drop-off at airport or hotel in Entebbe/Kampala

Departure Planning

Schedule flights for evening (after 6pm) from Entebbe. For early morning flights, we recommend booking an extra night in Entebbe.

Gorilla Trekking Preparation

Essential information for your 3-day express gorilla adventure

Essential Documents

  • • Valid passport (6+ months remaining)
  • • Uganda visa (available on arrival USD $50)
  • • Yellow fever vaccination certificate
  • • Travel insurance with medical evacuation
  • • Gorilla permit confirmation (we provide)

Permit Information

  • • Gorilla permit: USD $700 per person
  • • Permits included in tour price
  • • Must be booked months in advance
  • • Non-refundable once confirmed
  • • Minimum age: 15 years

Trekking Essentials

  • • Waterproof hiking boots (broken in)
  • • Garden gloves for vegetation
  • • Lightweight rain jacket/poncho
  • • Small daypack (waterproof)
  • • Walking sticks (provided)

Clothing

  • • Long-sleeved shirts (neutral colors)
  • • Long pants (quick-dry material)
  • • Warm layer (fleece/jacket)
  • • Hat with brim
  • • Extra socks (wool recommended)

Personal Items

  • • High SPF sunscreen
  • • Insect repellent (DEET based)
  • • Camera with extra batteries
  • • Personal medications
  • • Small first aid kit

Physical Requirements

  • • Moderate fitness level required
  • • Trekking duration: 2-6 hours
  • • Altitude: 1,190-2,607 meters
  • • Uneven, muddy terrain
  • • Can be physically demanding

Gorilla Trekking Rules

  • • Maximum 8 visitors per gorilla group
  • • Stay 7 meters from gorillas
  • • No flash photography
  • • Keep voices low at all times
  • • If sick (cold/flu), do not trek
  • • Follow guide instructions strictly
